# Flow reference videos: 12 new exercises

Each teammate takes a few of these, generates the clip in **Google Flow**, and uploads it to `videos/` with the **exact filename** below.
Until a file is uploaded, the app shows a "Reference video coming soon" placeholder for that exercise, so nothing breaks.

## Flow settings
- 16:9, highest quality, generate 2–4 variations and keep the one with the **cleanest form**
- Check for AI glitches: extra limbs, bending joints, wrong rep count, the person drifting out of frame
- Export as MP4. Keep it under ~10 MB (the existing four are about 2–3 MB, 10 seconds)

## Shared opening (paste at the start of every prompt)
> Static side-view camera at waist height, full body in frame with space around them. A fit young adult in plain dark athletic clothes, no logos, in a bright, clean, modern gym with a rubber floor. Soft even lighting. Slow, controlled, textbook-perfect form, like an instructional fitness video. No text, no music, quiet ambient gym sound.

## Prompts (add after the shared opening)

**1. `reverse-lunge.mp4`**
> The person performs two slow reverse lunges with the same leg. Starting upright, they take a long step backward and lower until both knees are bent at 90 degrees. The front knee stays directly over the ankle, the back knee lightly approaches the floor without touching hard, and the torso stays tall. They push through the front heel to return to standing.

**2. `goblet-squat.mp4`**
> The person holds one dumbbell vertically against their chest with both hands. They perform two slow squats: feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, hips back and down until thighs are parallel to the floor, elbows inside the knees at the bottom, chest proud, back flat, heels planted. They drive up to stand fully upright.

**3. `rdl.mp4`**
> The person holds a dumbbell in each hand in front of their thighs. They perform two slow Romanian deadlifts: soft bend in the knees, hips push straight back, back stays perfectly flat, dumbbells slide down close to the legs to just below the knees, then they squeeze the glutes to return to standing tall without leaning back.

**4. `step-up.mp4`**
> A sturdy flat bench is beside the person. They place one whole foot on the bench and perform two slow step-ups: driving through the heel of the foot on the bench to stand fully tall on top, torso upright, front knee in line with the toes, back foot only lightly assisting. They step down slowly with control.

**5. `glute-bridge.mp4`**
> The person lies on their back on a mat, knees bent, feet flat and hip-width apart near the hips, arms flat by their sides. They perform three slow glute bridges: squeezing the glutes to lift the hips until shoulders, hips, and knees form a straight line, ribs down with no lower-back arch, a brief pause at the top, then lowering slowly.

**6. `wall-sit.mp4`**
> A plain wall runs sideways to the camera. The person holds a steady wall sit for the whole clip: back and head flat against the wall, thighs parallel to the floor, knees bent at 90 degrees directly over the ankles, feet flat, arms relaxed at the sides. Only slight breathing movement.

**7. `incline-pushup.mp4`**
> A sturdy flat bench is in front of the person. They place their hands on the bench edge slightly wider than shoulders and perform three slow incline push-ups: body in one straight line from head to heels, elbows bending at about 45 degrees from the body, chest lowering to just above the bench edge, then pressing back to straight arms.

**8. `bench-dip.mp4`**
> The person sits on the edge of a sturdy bench, hands gripping the edge beside their hips, then slides forward with knees bent and feet flat on the floor. They perform three slow bench dips: hips staying close to the bench, elbows bending straight back to about 90 degrees, shoulders down away from the ears, then pressing back up to straight arms.

**9. `bent-over-row.mp4`**
> The person holds a dumbbell in each hand. They hinge at the hips with a flat back until the torso is at about 45 degrees, knees softly bent. They perform three slow rows: pulling the dumbbells toward the hips with elbows close to the body, squeezing the shoulder blades together at the top, then lowering with control to full arm extension. The back stays flat throughout.

**10. `overhead-press.mp4`**
> The person stands tall holding a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height, palms facing forward. They perform three slow overhead presses: pressing straight up until the arms are fully extended overhead with biceps by the ears, ribs down and glutes squeezed so the lower back does not arch, then lowering back to the shoulders with control.

**11. `mountain-climber.mp4`**
> The person starts in a high plank with hands directly under the shoulders and body in a straight line. They perform controlled mountain climbers at a moderate pace, alternating legs: each knee drives toward the chest while the hips stay level with the shoulders and do not pike up or sag.

**12. `bird-dog.mp4`**
> The person is on hands and knees on a mat, hands under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat. They perform two slow bird dogs on each side: extending one arm forward and the opposite leg straight back until both are level with the body, hips staying square to the floor, a brief pause, then returning to the start with control.
